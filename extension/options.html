CHROME SETUP

forgive-me.

Connect once. Review removals in your terminal.

1. Connect the terminal

Run forgive-me in your terminal. Keep it open.

The extension obtains the connection settings from the local app. You do not need to copy a secret.

Checking connection…

2. Sign in to X

Use this Chrome profile for your X account.

Waiting for your account.

3. Confirm your account

Return to the terminal. Make sure it shows the correct account.

Press Enter to open your followers. Press s to start the scan.

Connection does not approve any removals.

Manual connection and repair

If automatic pairing fails, restart the updated terminal app. Then retry the connection.

For manual pairing, press Shift+P in the terminal, then m.

If the terminal reports a missing X operation, refresh the X tab. Then refresh discovery.
